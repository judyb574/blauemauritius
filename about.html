About Us

Available 24 hours a day!

Available around the clock! The concept of Heavenly Holidays Travel & Tours and Blaue Mauritius as an international concierge service was launched in 2017 by entrepreneurs and professionals from the tourism, entertainment, and IT sectors from various countries. Our goal is to offer optimal service, especially to German-speaking visitors traveling to Mauritius, Sri Lanka, Malta, and other exclusive destinations, and to ensure maximum comfort before and during their stay – whether for vacation or business. Our concept emphasizes well-structured information, advice, and support from a team of communication professionals, utilizing modern technologies.
