Land Excursions: Mystical South

The South – a journey through the diverse landscapes of Mauritius

FULL-DAY EXPERIENCE – INCLUDING TRANSFER, GUIDE, LUNCH AND ENTRANCE FEES

Mystical South

Begin your journey at Trou aux Cerfs, an extinct volcanic crater offering a breathtaking 360° panoramic view over the island. Drive through the lush forested interior to Ganga Talao, a sacred lake highly revered by the Hindu community.

Continue your adventure in the Black River Gorges National Park, where you have lunch at a local restaurant overlooking the stunning coastline. Then visit Chamarel, with the impressive Chamarel Waterfall and the world-famous Seven Coloured Earth, a fascinating geological wonder.

On your way back, enjoy the impressive views of Le Morne mountain and Île aux Bénitiers – a perfect end to this unforgettable exploration of the diverse landscapes of southern Mauritius.

⚠ Note: The Chamarel region is known for its lush vegetation and rainy climate. We recommend bringing an umbrella, a raincoat or a light sweater, especially in the winter months.

Friendly reminder: don’t forget

  • Sun protection
  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Insect repellent

Highlights

Discover the most famous pilgrimage sites in Mauritius, including the sacred lake of Ganga Talao (Grand Bassin), then marvel at the unique Seven Coloured Earth of Chamarel, a breathtaking geological wonder of a special kind.

Did you know?

Standing an impressive 33 metres tall, the Shiva statue at Ganga Talao is one of the tallest statues of the god Shiva in the world, symbolising spiritual devotion and cultural heritage in Mauritius.
