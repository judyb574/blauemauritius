Nature Excursions: Tea Route

A journey through the heritage and flavours of Mauritius

FULL-DAY EXPERIENCE – INCLUDING TRANSFER, GUIDE, LUNCH, ENTRANCE FEES, TEA AND RUM TASTING

Tea Route

Start your day at Les Aubineaux, a colonial-era manor house dating from 1872 and a landmark of Mauritian culture. You then drive through picturesque tea fields to the Bois Chéri tea factory, where you discover the secrets of tea production, followed by a traditional tea break at the charming chalet.

Enjoy a delicious lunch at the historic Saint Aubin plantation house, dating from 1819, known for its locally grown vanilla, its anthurium greenhouse and its handcrafted rum.

The adventure continues at La Vanille Nature Park, home to the largest population of Aldabra tortoises and a wide variety of other wildlife. End your visit at the insectarium, with its extraordinary collection of more than 23,000 specimens – a truly immersive journey into the heritage, nature and flavours of Mauritius.

Friendly reminder: don’t forget

  • Umbrella or raincoat
  • Sun protection
  • Light jacket
  • Comfortable shoes

Highlights

Savour the rich aromas of locally produced tea and discover a variety of unique blends from Mauritius. Explore a rum distillery and discover the art and craftsmanship behind the island's finest rum. Don't miss the fascinating insectarium, with its extraordinary collection of more than 23,000 specimens, offering a unique insight into the diverse natural heritage of Mauritius.

Did you know?

La Vanille Nature Park has launched a long-term initiative to restore and recreate 50 hectares of native forest in the Ebony Forest, creating a vital refuge for the rare and endangered flora and fauna of Mauritius and preserving biodiversity for future generations.
