Nature Excursions: Odysseo

A glimpse into the wonders of our ocean

Odysseo

The oceanarium reflects the wonders of our seas and opens up new paths to knowledge, discovery and environmental awareness. It inspires visitors from all walks of life to take an active part in protecting our planet's marine ecosystems and safeguarding the future for generations to come.

At Odysseo, learning meets adventure – an interactive and educational experience where visitors can explore the secrets of the deep, interact with marine life and develop a deeper appreciation for our global environment. Here, curiosity is sparked, fun meets education and the beauty of the ocean comes to life.
