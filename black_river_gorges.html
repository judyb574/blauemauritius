Nature Excursions: Black River Gorges

Discover one of the largest nature parks in Mauritius

HALF-DAY EXPERIENCE – INCLUDING A CERTIFIED GUIDE

Hiking in the Black River Gorges

Set off on an easy to moderate forest hike along the Petrin trail, which leads to the breathtaking Black River Gorges, one of the largest nature parks in Mauritius.

This lush reserve is home to a wide variety of endemic, native and exotic plant species and offers a peaceful, immersive nature experience.

⚠ Not suitable for people with back or heart problems, limited mobility or dizziness, or after surgery.

Friendly reminder: don’t forget

  • Water
  • Extra clothes
  • Raincoat or light jacket
  • Insect repellent
  • Comfortable sports shoes
  • Sun protection gear
  • Backpack
