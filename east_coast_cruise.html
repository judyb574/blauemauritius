Sea Excursions: East Coast Cruise

A full day of adventure between sky and sea

FULL-DAY EXPERIENCE – INCLUDING TRANSFER, SNACKS, LUNCH, DRINKS, SWIMMING AND SNORKELLING

East Coast Cruise

Set off on a breathtaking cruise along the east coast of Mauritius, where the sheltered lagoon, the shimmering blue-green water and the stunning coastal scenery meet the majestic backdrop of towering mountains.

Feel the warmth of the sun and the refreshing sea breeze as you sail past historic sites – from the landing place of the first Dutch settlers to the legendary naval battle between the French and the English. A journey full of beauty, history and unforgettable memories awaits you!

Friendly reminder: don’t forget

  • Sun protection gear
  • Beach towel
  • Sun cream

Highlights

Your journey begins as you cast off at Trou d’Eau Douce and glide across crystal-clear water. Treat yourself to a refreshing swim in the sea, then enjoy a delicious lunch on board.

Relax on the pristine white sandy beaches of the famous Île aux Cerfs – where relaxation and nature come together for the perfect island getaway.
