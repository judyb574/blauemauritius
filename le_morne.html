Land Excursions: Le Morne Hiking & Climbing

An unforgettable eco-adventure for nature lovers

HALF-DAY EXPERIENCE – INCLUDING TRANSFER AND GUIDE

Le Morne Hiking & Climbing

Set off on an adventurous guided hike to the summit of the legendary Le Morne Brabant, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, 465 metres above sea level.

As you climb, enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of turquoise lagoons and lush mountain landscapes as far as the eye can see. During this four-hour hiking and climbing tour, discover the rich folklore and myths surrounding the history of this legendary mountain.

⚠ Not suitable for people with back or heart problems, limited mobility or dizziness, or after recent surgery.

Friendly reminder: don’t forget

  • Extra clothes
  • Insect repellent
  • Sun protection gear
  • Sun cream
  • Water
  • Hiking shoes and outfit
  • Backpack

Did you know?

Le Morne Brabant was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2008 and is a powerful memorial to the history of slavery in Mauritius. It symbolises resilience, freedom and the island's rich cultural heritage.
