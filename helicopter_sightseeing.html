Nature Excursions: Helicopter Sightseeing

Discover the breathtaking landscapes of Mauritius

15 MINUTES • 30 MINUTES • 45 MINUTES • 1 HOUR

Helicopter Sightseeing Flight

Take to the skies and discover the island's most breathtaking landscapes, including hidden and remote areas that can only be reached from the air. Soar over endless sugar cane fields, uniquely shaped mountain ranges, winding rivers, rushing waterfalls and lush valleys. Admire the pristine white beaches, crystal-clear lagoons, coral reefs and picturesque islets along the coast.

Fly over towns and historic sites and see the fascinating “underwater waterfall”, a breathtaking optical illusion created by shifting sandbanks and considered one of the most spectacular sights in the world. A once-in-a-lifetime flying adventure awaits you!
